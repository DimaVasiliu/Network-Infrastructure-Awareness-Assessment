## 2. The digital-content waiver explained

Under the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional
Charges) Regulations 2013 (UK), digital content normally comes with a 14-day
cancellation right. That right is **lost** as soon as:

1. you give express consent to the supply of the digital content beginning
   before the 14-day period ends, **and**
2. you acknowledge that you lose the cancellation right when the supply
   begins.

By tapping **"Buy" / "Install"** on the Apple App Store or Google Play, and
by allowing the App to download and run, you:

- expressly request immediate supply of the App, and
- acknowledge that you lose the 14-day right of withdrawal at that moment.

This is required for paid apps to work the way they do on the app stores.

## 3. When you can still get a refund

You may still be entitled to a full or partial refund in the following cases:

- **Faulty or non-functional App** — for example, the App does not run on a
  supported device, crashes on launch, or a feature paid for does not work.
  Statutory rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 (UK) apply.
- **Payment made in error** — for example, an accidental purchase by a child
  on a shared device, or a duplicate charge.
- **No access to the content paid for** — for example, the download never
  completed and reinstall does not fix it.

Where any of these apply, please follow section 4 below.

## 4. How to request a refund

### 4.1 Apple App Store

Apple processes refunds for App Store purchases. Use Apple's "Report a
Problem" tool:

- Web: https://reportaproblem.apple.com
- Sign in with the Apple ID used for the purchase
- Find the "Network Infrastructure Trainer" charge and choose a refund
  reason

Apple usually responds within 24–48 hours.

### 4.2 Google Play

Google processes refunds for Google Play purchases:

- Web: https://play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory
- Or in the Play Store app: Menu → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & order
  history → tap the order → Refund
- For purchases more than 48 hours old, fill out the request form linked
  from the order page.

Google usually responds within a few business days.

### 4.3 Contact us

If Apple or Google decline a refund that you believe is owed because the App
is faulty or because a payment was clearly made in error, contact us at
{{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} with:

- the email address used to buy the App,
- the date of purchase,
- the order ID or transaction reference,
- a short description of the issue (a screenshot or screen recording helps).

We will review and, where appropriate, support your refund request with the
store. We have no power to refund Apple or Google charges directly, but we
will not stand in the way of a legitimate refund.

## 5. What is **not** refundable

The following are not, on their own, grounds for a refund:

- You changed your mind after downloading the App.
- You did not pass the assessment after using the App.
- You decided not to take the assessment.
- You bought the App on the wrong account.
- You shared an account with someone else and they made the purchase.

This list is illustrative, not exhaustive, and does not affect any statutory
rights you have as a consumer.

## 6. Chargebacks

contacting Apple, Google or us may result in your access to the App being
suspended. Please use the steps in section 4 first.
