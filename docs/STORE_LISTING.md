### Description

> Use the same text on both Apple and Google. Both stores allow this length.

```
Network Infrastructure Trainer is an independent offline study aid for the UK Network Infrastructure Awareness Assessment.

WHAT YOU GET
• 155 original practice questions across all nine assessment topics: Product Selection, Containment Systems, Cable Laying, Cable Dressing, Fire Regulations, Safe Cable Installation, Personal Safety, Other Services and Waste Management.
• Mock-exam mode that follows the published format: 30 questions, 45-minute timer, pass mark 24 correct (80%), weighted across the same topics as the real assessment.
• Practice by section with explanations and a reference to the relevant standard or regulation for every question (BS EN 50173-1, BS EN 50174-2, BS EN 50174-3, BS 7671, HD 60364-5-54, Construction Products Regulations, Work at Height Regulations 2005, COSHH 2002, and more).
• Focus modes: review your wrong answers, your bookmarked questions and your ten weakest topics in one tap.
• Per-section accuracy on the Stats screen so you can see exactly where to focus.

PRIVACY-FIRST
• Works fully offline.
• No tracking, no advertising, no analytics SDKs.
• All progress, bookmarks and stats stay on your device. Clear them at any time.

WHO IT IS FOR
Electricians, telecoms installers, datacomms apprentices and anyone preparing for the UK Network Infrastructure Awareness Assessment.

IMPORTANT — INDEPENDENT STUDY AID
This app is an independent study aid. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by The JIB or the Electrotechnical Certification Scheme. "ECS" is a trademark of The JIB. The app does not include any copyrighted question content from the official assessment guide; all questions and explanations are original and based on publicly available standards and regulations.

Standards change. Always verify current requirements before relying on them at work. This app is a study aid, not professional advice.

SUPPORT
Email support@timrx.live for questions, refund requests or feedback.

PRIVACY
Read the full privacy policy at https://timrx.live/legal/nia/privacy.
```
